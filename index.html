Privacy

What we store, who can see it, and the controls you have:

  • Account: your email (used for sign-in magic links) and your chosen username (changeable once every 30 days).
  • Profile: your bio, avatar, and Top 8 Buns showcase are visible to any signed-in user who views your profile.
  • Your collection / wish list / pen counts / nicknames / meanings / acquired-how: stored in our database. Each item carries a visibility setting (Public → Coven → Castle Crew → Coffin Buddies → Only me); new items default to friends.
  • Posts, comments, likes, reactions, @mentions: shown to others based on the visibility you choose for each post. Anyone who can see a post can see its comments, likes, and who reacted. People you @mention are linked and may be notified.
  • Friends & circles: your Coven (friends) and your directed circles (Castle Crew, Coffin Buddies) are stored; the other person can tell when you've added them.
  • Photos: stored in our cloud storage. Note, and a change from before: photos on social posts and trade offerings are viewable by any signed-in user (that's how the feed and Browse work). For those, your visibility tier protects the post text, but the image itself is shielded only by an unguessable link — treat social/trade photos as effectively shareable. Photos kept inside your private collection stay scoped to you and anyone you've shared a collection with.
  • Trade offerings: publicly browsable by other signed-in users (that's the point — they need to see what you're willing to trade).
  • Shipping addresses: stored as structured fields; revealed only to the other party of an accepted trade, and only after they've also entered theirs.
  • Feedback: the ratings (communication / shipping / accuracy, plus comments) you give or receive are visible to other signed-in users via your profile. You can edit your own feedback for 7 days.
  • Notifications: today these fire in your browser while the app is open. If we ship native apps, we'll store a device push token to deliver them.
  • Blocking & reporting: you can block another collector — you'll be hidden from each other, any friendship is removed, and neither of you can friend or trade with the other. You can also report a post, comment, or collector to the admins. Reports are private (only you and the admins see them), and we never tell someone they've been blocked or reported.
  • Admins can read collection / wish list / trade data and remove social content for moderation. Currently the only admin is the site operator.
  • Third parties: auth, database, and storage run on Supabase; magic-link emails send via our SMTP provider (Resend); image proxying, photo storage, and hosting run on Cloudflare; catalog data is fetched from plushiedreadfuls.com. We don't sell your data.
  • Deletion: email support@plushcrypt.com from your account address and we'll wipe your rows and photos (backups age out afterward).

It's per-device, changes nothing for anyone else, and switching it off restores everything.

About

The Plush Crypt is an independent, fan-made home for plushie collectors — catalog what you own, keep a wish list, trade with other collectors, and share it all through a small social layer of friends, posts, and your Top 8 Buns. Simple tools — blocking and reporting — help keep the crypt kind.

We're not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Plushie Dreadfuls Co. or any other plushie manufacturer. Product names, photos, and trademarks belong to their respective owners.
