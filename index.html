  • No warranty. The service is provided as-is. We don't guarantee uptime, data preservation, or that anything works.
  • You're responsible for what you list. Don't list counterfeit goods or anything illegal to ship to or from your jurisdiction.
  • Trades are between you and the other person. We don't escrow, mediate, verify identity, or guarantee anything. Use the feedback system and the ship-first guidance to manage risk.
  • You're responsible for the addresses you share. Only enter a real shipping address when you trust the other party enough.
  • Don't abuse the service. We may suspend accounts that defraud other users, harass others, or misuse the platform.
  • We may change or shut down the service at any time, with or without notice. Your data is your responsibility — you can request a copy or deletion by emailing support@plushcrypt.com.
  • Catalog data is fetched live from public product feeds (currently plushiedreadfuls.com). We don't claim ownership of catalog content or images.

Privacy

What we store and who can see it:

  • Account: your email (used for sign-in magic links) and your chosen username.
  • Your collection / wish list / pen counts / trade items / nickname / meaning / acquired-how: stored in our database. Only visible to you and anyone you've shared your collection with.
  • Photos: stored in our cloud storage, scoped to your collection. Other users can't browse them.
  • Trade offerings: publicly browsable by other signed-in users (that's the point — they need to see what you're willing to trade).
  • Shipping addresses: revealed only to the other party of an accepted trade, and only after they've also entered theirs.
  • Feedback: the ratings (good / meh / bad) and comments you give or receive are visible to other signed-in users via your profile.
  • Admins can read collection / wish list / trade data for moderation. Currently the only admin is the site operator.
  • Third parties: auth, database, storage, and transactional email run on Supabase. Magic-link emails send via our SMTP provider (Resend). Catalog data is fetched from plushiedreadfuls.com. We don't sell your data.
  • Deletion: email support@plushcrypt.com from your account address and we'll wipe your rows and photos.

About

The Plush Crypt is an independent fan-made tracker built so plushie collectors can catalog what they own, what they want, and trade with other collectors.
